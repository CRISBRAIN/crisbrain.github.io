CRISBRAIN Decision — Distribution Partners

External distribution is open. CRISBRAIN is an independent commercial decision-intelligence publisher operating across 11 markets. Our public pages help users reduce purchase risk before choosing a product.

We are looking for publishers, aggregators, affiliate/distribution networks, creator networks, comparison platforms and other partners that already have audience and can syndicate CRISBRAIN decision links.

Distribution model

Partner / aggregator → CRISBRAIN decision page → explicit user decision → governed commercial destination.

The proprietary reasoning core, commercial credentials and destination governance remain private on the CRISBRAIN server. Partners distribute public CRISBRAIN pages; they do not receive or execute the private decision engine.

Partner access

Human partners use one entry point to review the 11 markets and their public CRISBRAIN publications.

Open distribution catalog

RSS/XML is kept only inside the catalog as a separate technical option for platforms and automated syndication.

Why distribute CRISBRAIN

  • Decision-oriented content instead of generic product-link duplication.
  • Market-specific public distribution across 11 markets.
  • Server-side governance keeps the proprietary core and commercial routing private.
  • Partner traffic can be attributed without exposing affiliate credentials.
  • Commercial outcomes can be measured by CRISBRAIN MONEY as evidence accumulates.
