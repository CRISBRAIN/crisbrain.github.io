CRISBRAIN Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

CRISBRAIN is a decision-governance platform designed to help identify, organize, evaluate and distribute commercial decision content.

Information We Process

CRISBRAIN may process public product-page information and minimum technical data necessary for security, authentication, operation and service improvement.

Pinterest Integration

CRISBRAIN may connect to Pinterest through Pinterest's official OAuth authorization process.

When a user authorizes CRISBRAIN, Pinterest provides an OAuth access token that allows CRISBRAIN to perform only the actions approved by the user through the permissions granted during authorization.

CRISBRAIN does not request, collect or store the user's Pinterest password.

Depending on the permissions granted, CRISBRAIN may access information required to:

  • read Pinterest boards;
  • create or manage boards;
  • read Pins;
  • create or manage Pins;
  • access basic authorized Pinterest account information;
  • retrieve information necessary to operate and verify the Pinterest integration.

Pinterest Data We May Process

  • Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens;
  • Pinterest account and authorization identifiers;
  • board identifiers and names;
  • Pin identifiers and publication information;
  • API response and operational status information;
  • technical audit records necessary to verify authorized actions.

CRISBRAIN only processes Pinterest information required to provide and operate the authorized integration.

How Pinterest Information Is Used

  • authenticate the authorized Pinterest integration;
  • publish or manage Pinterest content when authorized;
  • verify successful API operations;
  • prevent duplicate or unauthorized actions;
  • maintain operational and security audit records;
  • measure authorized distribution activity.

Pinterest information is not sold.

OAuth Credentials and Security

OAuth credentials are handled server-side and are not intentionally exposed through public pages or client-side application interfaces.

CRISBRAIN uses technical controls intended to restrict commercial write operations to authorized runtime services and to prevent unauthorized publication actions.

Google Search Console

When authorized by the account holder, CRISBRAIN C.M.C. may access Google Search Console data to review search performance, sitemaps and URL inspection status. These data are used as discovery and market-governance evidence and are not automatically treated as proof of sale.

Financial Data and Purchases

CRISBRAIN does not request card numbers, does not process payments and does not execute purchases on behalf of users.

Sharing of Information

CRISBRAIN does not sell personal information or Pinterest user data.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as necessary to operate CRISBRAIN's technical services, including application hosting and persistent data storage.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Data Retention

OAuth credentials and integration records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain the authorized integration, provide the service, meet security or audit requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Authorization credentials may be revoked or replaced when Pinterest access is disconnected or reauthorized.

User Control

Users remain in control of their Pinterest authorization and may revoke CRISBRAIN's access through Pinterest account settings where supported by Pinterest.

Revoking authorization prevents CRISBRAIN from making subsequent authenticated Pinterest API requests using the revoked authorization.

Third-Party Services

Pinterest and Google are independent third-party services. Their use is also governed by their own terms, policies and privacy practices.

CRISBRAIN accesses connected services only through authorized interfaces and APIs.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in CRISBRAIN functionality, legal requirements or third-party integrations. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Privacy-related requests regarding CRISBRAIN may be sent through Contact.
